THE IDEA BEHIND STILLPOINT

A point of light.
A space to choose.

In Brahma Kumaris’ Raja Yoga philosophy, the soul is understood as a conscious spiritual being, often visualised as a point of light. Practising soul consciousness means remembering the inner self beyond our roles.

This student game adapts three ideas: step back as a detached observer, notice the quality of a thought, and choose a peaceful, purposeful response. The light represents awareness; it does not erase the situation.

In this game, positive thoughts are kind and realistic, necessary thoughts help the next action, negative thoughts judge or predict harm, and unnecessary thoughts repeat without helping. These are learning categories; a real thought may overlap.

Feelings are welcome here. A constructive thought can acknowledge stress and still help us act.
